Port Scanning (How Nmap Works)

Checking every door of a building to see which are open. Press Next step.

What just happened?

The step you are on lights up here.

  1. 0. Ports – Ports are numbered entrances to services on a computer.
  2. 1. Reconnaissance – Attackers map the target first. Nmap is the standard tool.
  3. 2. Open port – A reply means a service is listening.
  4. 3. Mapping – Open and closed ports show what the machine offers.
  5. 4. Version detection – Banners often reveal the exact software and version.
  6. 5. Exploit lookup – Old versions have public, known exploits.
  7. 6. Close unused ports – Every open service is attack surface. Turn off what you do not use.
  8. 7. Firewall and patches – Restrict access, update software and monitor logs for scans.

Why it matters

Port scanning is the first step of most attacks and also of most security audits and penetration tests.

Rule: only scan systems you own or have written permission to test. Scanning others can be illegal.
