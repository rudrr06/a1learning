Zero Trust: Never Trust, Always Verify

Show your ID at every door, even inside the building. Press Next step.

What just happened?

The step you are on lights up here.

  1. 0. Perimeter model – Trust everything inside the network, defend only the edge.
  2. 1. Weakness – One breach lets an attacker move laterally to everything.
  3. 2. Zero trust – Never trust by location; verify each request.
  4. 3. What is checked – Identity, device health, context and need.
  5. 4. Least privilege – Give only the access the job needs.
  6. 5. Continuous – Access is re-evaluated and revoked when risk changes.
  7. 6. Segmentation – Assume breach and keep the damage small.
  8. 7. A mindset – Built from identity, devices, least privilege and monitoring.

Why it matters

Zero trust is now the standard thinking for modern companies because staff work from everywhere and attackers often get in with stolen logins.

Rule: location is not trust. Prove identity, check the device, grant the minimum, and watch continuously.
