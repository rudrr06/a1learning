Social Engineering: Hacking People

The easiest lock to pick is a helpful person. Press Next step.

What just happened?

The step you are on lights up here.

  1. 0. Hacking people – The weakest link is often trust, not technology.
  2. 1. Research – Attackers gather public information first (OSINT).
  3. 2. Pretext – A believable story plus authority and urgency.
  4. 3. Pressure – Rushed people skip verification.
  5. 4. The ask – Often a password, an MFA code or a "quick" action.
  6. 5. Breach – Technical defences are bypassed by human error.
  7. 6. Defence – Stop, verify through a known channel, report.
  8. 7. Many channels – Email, phone, SMS and in person all work the same way.

Why it matters

Social engineering is behind a large share of breaches because it is cheaper than finding a software flaw.

Rule: verify unusual requests through a second, trusted channel. Feeling rushed is a warning sign.
