VPN: The Encrypted Tunnel

A private tube through a public street. Press Next step.

What just happened?

The step you are on lights up here.

  1. 0. No VPN – Local networks and sites see where you go and your real IP.
  2. 1. Connect – The VPN client builds an encrypted tunnel to a VPN server.
  3. 2. Wrap – Traffic is encrypted before leaving your device.
  4. 3. Local view – The Wi-Fi owner sees only an encrypted stream.
  5. 4. Forward – The VPN server sends requests to sites for you.
  6. 5. New IP – Sites see the VPN server's IP, not yours.
  7. 6. Limits – The VPN provider sees your traffic; a VPN is not anonymity or antivirus.
  8. 7. Good uses – Public Wi-Fi, remote work and ISP privacy, with a trusted provider.

Why it matters

VPNs are used by almost every company to give remote staff safe access, and by many people on public Wi-Fi.

Rule: a VPN moves trust from the network to the VPN provider. Choose one you trust.
